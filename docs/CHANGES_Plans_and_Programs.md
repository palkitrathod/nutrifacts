# Plans & Programs: What Changed and Why

October 2026

## Summary

- Three programs and one category were **folded into Nutrition Care** as goals (nothing a customer could buy was lost).
- The first consultation now has **one price everywhere: ₹499**.
- Condition programs can now be **chosen and paid for online**.

---

## 1. Removed

### Category: Everyday Wellness

| Removed program | Where it went |
|---|---|
| Lifestyle Reset | Nutrition Care → goal **"Better habits"** |
| Weight Management | Nutrition Care → goal **"Weight goal"** |
| Women's Wellness | Nutrition Care → goal **"Women's wellness"** |

**Why:**
- They had the same price as Nutrition Care (₹2,499 a month, ₹5,999 for 3 months) and much the same contents, so the site was showing the same offer twice under different names.
- That makes choosing harder: a visitor had to compare four similar ₹2,499 options.
- One plan with a goal choice is simpler, and each goal still shows its own highlights.

### Old price: First Consultation ₹500

Replaced by **₹499** (see section 2).

**Why:** the booking pop-up said ₹500 while the plans said ₹499. One price everywhere is clearer and more honest.

---

## 2. Added / changed

### Nutrition Care: "What's your goal?"
- The Nutrition Care card (1, 3 or 6 months) asks the customer to pick a goal: Better habits, Weight goal or Women's wellness.
- Each goal shows its own "For your goal" highlights.
- The chosen goal is saved with the payment, shown at checkout and printed on the receipt.
- The server only accepts one of these three goals.

**Why:** customers still see a plan that speaks to their own goal, without the clinic running three copies of the same plan.

### First Consultation ₹499, paid online
- **No plan yet:** booking the first consultation goes to checkout ("Continue to Pay ₹499"). The appointment is booked once the payment succeeds.
- **The ₹499 is credited in full** if the customer moves up to a bigger plan within 14 days.
- **Plan running:** consultations show "Included in your plan", at ₹0.
- **Follow-ups without a plan:** still ₹300, paid at the session (unchanged).

**Why:** a single, clear entry price. The clinic is paid for its time, and the customer loses nothing if they continue.

### Condition programs bought online
- Every priced program has a **"Choose · ₹X"** button for the length picked (1, 3 or 6 months), which opens checkout.
- **My Plans** has a new "Condition Programs" section:
  - With no plan running: buy directly.
  - With a plan running: upgrade with the unused days credited, or switch when the current plan ends.
- **Oncology, Pancreatic Health, Enteral & Parenteral Nutrition** stay "assessment first": the button books the First Consultation, and the price is quoted after it.
- **The dietitian** can see, re-price and switch on/off every program and length.

**Why:** customers can decide for themselves: pay online straight away, or consult first. All prices are published, in line with full transparency.

### Smaller fixes
- **Refund suggestion:** consultations already used are now valued at the standard rates (₹499 first, ₹300 follow-up). Before, sessions included in a plan counted as ₹0, which overstated the refund.
- **Program cards:** a program without an online price no longer breaks the page. It shows "Personalised plan & quote" and offers the First Consultation.

## 4. Open points (not changed yet)

- **GST wording on the receipt.** It still says "TAX INVOICE" and shows a GSTIN and a "GST 0%" line, but the clinic does not have GST. This should be removed.
